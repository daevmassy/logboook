---
name: Supervisor bootstrap
description: How Daybook initializes and protects supervisor access.
---

The first account to complete a profile becomes the initial supervisor. Later accounts start as employees; only supervisors can grant or revoke supervisor access, and the app must retain at least one supervisor. Do not let sign-ups select their own role.

**Why:** A new team needs someone who can administer access, while public role selection would let any new account grant itself privileges.

**How to apply:** Preserve this bootstrap rule and the last-supervisor safeguard when changing onboarding or team access. If replacing them, use an explicit trusted provisioning path.
